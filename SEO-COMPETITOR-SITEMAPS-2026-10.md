# Competitor sitemap audit, 2026-10-02

Method: the "competitor sitemap" playbook (digiassets.co.il). Pulled public sitemaps of 9 San Diego
garage door companies that rank organically (about 1,100 URLs), clustered URLs by topic, and checked
recency (lastmod since 2026-06-01). No paid traffic tool, so step 5 (traffic validation) used OUR
Search Console data instead of competitor traffic estimates. Lastmod dates are a weak signal.

Sites: coastalgaragedoors.com (111), doordorks.com (182), sandiegodoorpros.com (119), acedoorco.com (10),
doorandrollgaragedoor.com (118), safetygaragedoor.com (131), garageprossandiego.com (57),
rsgaragedoorservices.com (173), callgoodguys.com (221). No sitemap: garagedoorssandiego.net, castlegaragedoors.com.

## Our own Search Console (60 days to 2026-09-29)

- /garage-door-springs/ 2,342 impr @ 17.8, /new-garage-door/ 1,023 impr @ 26.2: demand is there, rank is not.
- Chula Vista city post 254 impr @ 9.5 (city format CAN work). La Jolla 23 impr @ 44.6 (it does not always).
- Most posts have under 30 impressions. Overall the site still has an authority gap, not a content gap
  (see SEO-ROADMAP.md section 1): more pages alone will not fix rankings without GBP strength and links.

## Actions taken

1. TOPIC_QUEUE: new-door cluster (old Tier 5) moved ahead of Tier 4 diagnostics. Next posts:
   San Marcos (already next, 7 of 9 competitors have it), then new-garage-door-cost-san-diego,
   signs-you-need-a-new-garage-door, and the rest of the new-door cluster.
2. City pages: no change to roadmap rule 4. Reinstate parked cities (Solana Beach, Del Mar, Coronado,
   Scripps Ranch, Rancho Bernardo) only after San Marcos / Spring Valley / National City pass the
   90-day test. Competitors' heavy city investment is a hypothesis, not proof; their domains are older.

## Rules (from the playbook)
Take topics, formats and cadence. Never copy headlines, structure or text. Every post must carry
something competitors cannot: Noah's real job photos, real prices from the canonical list, real reviews.
